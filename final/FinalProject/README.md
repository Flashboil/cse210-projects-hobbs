# HOW TO PLAY "CLASS3S"

This is an explanation of the board and how it works.

# SCORE
You - Opponent : Score
0 = 0 : 0

Here you will see the score for the game. The damage you have dealt to your oponent
is on the left, and the damage your oponent has been dealt to you is on the right.
When the difference beteen the two scores is greater than 10, the game will end.

This is the board. You play your cards on the bottom row. There are five columns or "lanes"
that you can play in. You may not play a card where another card has already been played.
Your oponent will play cards in the topmost row. This is the "preview row". Here you can get
a heads up what card is coming and place blockers accordingly. Cards in the preview row do not
attack during combat. They will move into the second row, the oponent's main row, the next turn.

# BATTERY
[+][_][/][/][/][/]

You play cards using battery from this meter. You start out having only one battery to spend,
but each turn you will get one more until you reach a maximum of six. You battery is fully replenished each turn to its current max. Available battery is displayed as "[+]" and used
battery is dispalyed as "[_]". Locked battery slots are displayed as "[/]"

# CARDS
3───┐
|2/3|
└───┘

A card has a few different numbers on it. The number in the top left represents the battery cost
for the card. You can only play the card if you have enough availabl battery. 
The number on the left in the center is the card's attack power. During combat, the card will deal
damage equal to it's power to the opposing card or your oponent.
The number on the right in the center is the card's health. You card can take damage equal to it's
health before it is destroyed and removed from the board. A card will block all damage, even excess
damage from the oposing card.

You can place cards in front of other cards to block their attack. In this example,
An enemy card (2/3) is opposing the player card (1/2). During combat, the player's card will deal
1 damage to the opposing card, reducing it to a (2/2). No damage will be dealt to the oponent.
In the enemy combat turn, the opponent's card will deal 2 damage to the player's card, reducing it to a (1/0), destroying it. Still, no damage will be dealt by the card to the player.
